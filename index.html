🐾 welcome to the department

For study, not for sale. Everything here is shared only for personal, non-commercial learning among zoology students. Please don't resell, rebrand, or re-upload this material elsewhere — credit the source if you pass it on.

Our batch WhatsApp group keeps losing notes in the scroll every semester, so I put this together as one place everyone can actually find things again — and add to. It's organised the way the official syllabus is: by semester, then by paper. Not official, just useful. — Vikramaditya

Semester I

Non-Chordata & Chordata · Entomology & Fish Biology · Biochemical & Molecular Techniques · Biochemistry & Molecular Biology

Tap a paper to see what's on the shelf for it, and to add a Drive link or file of your own.

Semester II

Cytogenetics & Genetics · Vertebrate Endocrinology · Mammalian Physiology & Neurobiology · Cell Biology & Histology · Biostatistics & Bioinformatics

Includes the Semester II minor elective, Evolutionary Biology.

Semester III

Developmental Biology & Evolution · Immunology & Parasitology · Major Elective (streams A–E)

Elective streams: A Biochemistry & Molecular Biology, B Entomology, C Fish Biology, D Molecular Endocrinology & Mammalian Reproductive Physiology, E Molecular & Human Genetics. Includes the minor elective, Hormones and Diseases.

Semester IV

Animal Behaviour & Environmental Biology · Major Elective continued · Project & Seminar

Same five elective streams as Semester III, continued as Courses III & IV, plus dissertation and seminar.
